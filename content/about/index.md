Bullish Ideas is a place to examine what might be possible with design and technology.

This site publishes working ideas and essays. It does not present proposals as finished products or completed projects. Each piece states its premise and shows where more evidence would change the view.

The writing is for people who make, study, or use digital tools. It aims to connect a clear question with practical details: what to notice, what to try, and what a result could mean.

## How to read these ideas

Some pieces are essays. Others are proposals that need discussion or testing. The page will say which kind it is. Treat a proposal as a starting point, not a claim that a solution already works.

Bullish Ideas will focus on design and technology. The exact subjects can grow through reading, experiments, and questions from visitors.
