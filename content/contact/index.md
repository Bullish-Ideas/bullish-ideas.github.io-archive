Bullish Ideas has no verified public email address or form endpoint. The contact form stays unavailable until a real service can receive submissions.

To suggest a subject or challenge a published idea, [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/Bullish-Ideas/bullish-ideas.github.io/issues/new). Include the page link and a clear question. GitHub will ask you to sign in.
